Wirtshaus covers my Mac – playing cards and bits and pieces above everything
============================================================================
(Deutsch: siehe "Zum Installieren.txt")

Install – the fastest way (1 minute, only once)

1. Drag Wirtshausonmac.app into the Applications folder.

2. Open Terminal:
   press cmd + space, type "Terminal", press Enter.

3. Copy this line, paste it into Terminal (cmd + V) and press Enter:

   xattr -dr com.apple.quarantine /Applications/Wirtshausonmac.app

   Nothing is printed – that's how it should be. You can close Terminal.

4. Double-click Wirtshausonmac.app. Done.

Why this is needed
Everything that reaches your Mac via WhatsApp, AirDrop, mail or a browser is
put into "quarantine" by macOS. For apps that aren't registered with Apple
(that costs the developer 99 € a year), macOS then refuses to open them with
"Apple could not verify ..." or "... is damaged".
The line from step 3 only removes that mark from Wirtshausonmac – it changes
nothing else on your Mac.

Without Terminal (takes a little longer):
Double-click Wirtshausonmac once and close the warning with "Done".
Then open System Settings > Privacy & Security, scroll all the way down,
click "Open Anyway" next to Wirtshausonmac and confirm with your Mac password.
(If the message says "is damaged", only the Terminal way works.)

How to use it
- The app lives in the menu bar (two-cards icon).
  In full screen: move the mouse to the top edge of the screen and it appears.
- Drag a card = move it
- Double-click = flip it
- Right-click = menu (pick a card, back, size, remove …)
- Scroll over the card / pinch with two fingers = change the size
- Turn with two fingers or ⌥ + scroll = rotate
  (also with the "Rotate" slider; "Straighten" puts it upright again)
- "Size" slider in the menu and in the settings (8 to 900 pt)
- Switch decks: French (Skat: jacks and aces) or
  Bavarian (Schafkopf: Ober, Unter and Sows – der Alte, der Blaue,
  die Blaue, die Hundsgfickte …)
- Add: beer stein (foams), cigarette (smokes), bratwurst and
  coffee (steam), pretzel. You can click through smoke and steam.
- Language: German when your Mac is set to German, English otherwise.

Anywhere, even in full screen:
- ⌃⌥⌘K  show/hide everything
- ⌃⌥⌘L  lock on/off – locked cards let every click through to the
         app below and can't be moved

Runs on macOS 14 (Sonoma) or newer, on Apple silicon and Intel Macs.
